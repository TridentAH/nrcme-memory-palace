The 13 standards — 49 CFR 391.41(b)

# Standard # Standard
1 Loss of foot/leg/hand/arm (or SPE) 8 Epilepsy / LOC — objective standard
2 Limb impairment affecting prehension/power grasp (or SPE) 9 Mental / psychiatric disorder
3 Insulin-treated diabetes (must meet 391.46) 10 Vision — objective standard
4 Cardiovascular (MI, angina, coronary insufficiency, thrombosis; syncope/dyspnea/collapse/CHF) 11 Hearing — objective standard
5 Respiratory dysfunction 12 Drug use (Schedule I = never; II–V need prescription exception)

7 Rheumatic/arthritic/orthopedic/muscular/neuromuscular/vascular

Hard thresholds

Item Number
Vision acuity 20/40 each eye AND both eyes, corrected OK
Visual field 70° horizontal meridian, each eye
Colors Red, green, amber
Hearing — whisper 5 ft, better ear, aid OK
Hearing — audiometry Avg loss ≤ 40 dB at 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 Hz, better ear (ANSI Z24.5-1951)
BP Stage 1 140–159 / 90–99 → 1 yr
BP Stage 2 160–179 / 100–109 → one-time 3 months, then 1 yr if ≤ 140/90
BP Stage 3 ≥ 180/110 → disqualify; when ≤ 140/90 → 6 months, then every 6 months
Treated HTN, controlled max 1 yr (never 2)
ETT "satisfactory" > 6 METs (Bruce stage 2), HR ≥ 85% predicted, SBP rise ≥ 20, no angina/ST changes
LVEF ≥ 40% to certify (MI, CABG, HF) [legacy]; band labels (55–70 normal … < 35 severely low) [clinical]
Asthma FEV1 no cert if < 65% predicted
Pulse ox / ABG SpO2 < 92% → ABG; PaO2 < 65 mm Hg (< 5,000 ft) or PaCO2 > 45 → may not meet
Urine SG normal 1.005–1.030 [clinical]
AUDIT / CAGE AUDIT ≥ 8; CAGE ≥ 2 [clinical — FMCSA mandates neither]
CBD must be < 0.3% THC; marijuana = Schedule I

Waiting periods

Heads-up: these come from the older Appendix A / Cardiovascular Advisory Panel tables that most NRCME training courses and practice banks still use. The 2024 ME Handbook dropped fixed waiting periods and 1-year cardiac intervals in favor of individual assessment ("up to 2 years"). Regulation-based numbers (HTN stages, seizures, ITDM, vision) are unchanged. See the last section.

Condition Wait
MI 2 months (ETT at 4–6 wks; repeat every 2 yrs)
Angina (stable) 3 months free of rest angina / pattern change
PCI (elective, uncomplicated) 1 week per FMCSA's 2002 Cardiovascular Advisory Panel guidance; many training courses teach 3 weeks — be ready for either
CABG 3 months
Pacemaker 3 months (syncope) — remedial, not disqualifying
ICD never certify
AF / SVT / ventricular arrhythmia (treated) 1 month
Valve repair/replacement, aneurysm repair, PVD surgery, PE 3 months
Neurocardiogenic syncope 3 months after treatment
Epilepsy 10 yrs seizure-free off meds (exemption: 8 yrs, meds stable 2 yrs → 1-yr cert)
Single unprovoked seizure 5 yrs off meds (exemption: 4 yrs → 2-yr cert)
Provoked seizure none once resolved
LOC, unknown cause, not seizure 6 months
TIA / stroke (mild) 1 yr; cortical/subcortical stroke 5 yrs
Mild TBI (no LOC) 1 month; moderate TBI 3 yrs; severe (LOC ≥ 24 h) → do not certify
Bacterial meningitis w/ early seizure 5 yrs; viral encephalitis w/ early seizure 10 yrs
Benign CNS tumor post-op 1 yr (infratentorial); 2 yrs (supratentorial)
Vertigo (BPPV) 2 months symptom-free
Major depression (non-psychotic, no suicidality) 6 months symptom-free; severe / suicide attempt / mania → 1 yr
Schizophreniform / brief reactive psychosis 6 months; other psychoses 1 yr; schizophrenia dx → do not certify
ECT 6 months symptom-free, not on maintenance
Sedating med / antihistamine / narcotic antitussive no driving for 12 h
ITDM, hypoglycemia severe hypoglycemic episode (needs assistance / LOC / seizure / coma) → driving prohibited until the treating clinician confirms the cause is addressed, regimen is stable, and completes a new MCSA-5870 (391.46(e)); the form asks about the preceding 3 months — many training courses treat "an episode within the last 12 months" as disqualifying

Certification intervals

Situation Max
Everything normal 2 yrs (never longer)
Insulin-treated DM (391.46) 12 months; only 3 months if < 3 months of glucose records
Alternative Vision Standard (391.44) 1 yr
Hearing exemption, hearing aid 2 yrs
Seizure exemption epilepsy 1 yr; single unprovoked 2 yrs
SPE certificate 2 yrs (SPE itself valid 2 yrs; renew 30 days before)
Most cardiac conditions 1 yr
Chronic venous disease, varicose veins, autonomic neuropathy 2 yrs
Periodic-monitoring boxes 3 mo / 6 mo / 1 yr / other
