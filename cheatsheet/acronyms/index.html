Acronym Glossary

Every abbreviation used in this guide, A–Z. FMCSA form numbers are listed together at the end.

Acronym Stands for What it means for the DOT exam
AAA Abdominal aortic aneurysm Rupture risk tracks size; pulsatile mass → DQ pending imaging/CV consult
AASM American Academy of Sleep Medicine Source of the optional OSA screening criteria (neck size, BMI, Mallampati)
ABG Arterial blood gas Requested when SpO2 < 92%; PaO2 < 65 (or < 50 above 5,000 ft) or PaCO2 > 45 may fail the respiratory standard
ACE inhibitor Angiotensin-converting-enzyme inhibitor Antihypertensive class; side effects hypotension/syncope
ADD / ADHD Attention deficit (hyperactivity) disorder Case-by-case; stimulants are Schedule II → prescription exception, verify adult diagnosis
AF / A-fib Atrial fibrillation Decision hinges on underlying heart disease; anticoagulation OK if monitored
AHI Apnea–hypopnea index OSA severity: mild ≥ 5/h, moderate ≥ 15/h, severe > 30/h
ALS Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis Motor neuron disease; training courses often teach as disqualifying, MEH says case-by-case
ANSI (Z24.5-1951) American National Standards Institute Calibration standard for audiometry in the hearing regulation (not ISO)
AR / AS Aortic regurgitation / aortic stenosis Valve disease; severe AS not certifiable until repaired
AUDIT Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test WHO 10-item screen; score ≥ 8 = hazardous use
AV Atrioventricular (node/block) or aortic valve Context-dependent: AV block → pacemaker; AV repair → 3-month wait (old guidance)
AVM Arteriovenous malformation Brain vascular lesion; seizure/LOC risk assessed under (b)(8)
AVS Alternative Vision Standard (49 CFR 391.44) Replaced the vision exemption in 2022; MCSA-5871; 1-year cert
BKA Below-knee amputation Limb loss → needs SPE certificate; with SPE may certify 2 years
BMI Body mass index OSA risk factor (> 35 in AASM screen)
BP Blood pressure Staged 1/2/3 → 1 yr / one-time 3 mo / DQ until ≤ 140/90
BPPV / BPV Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo Old guidance: 2 months symptom-free; meclizine sedates
BUN Blood urea nitrogen Renal function lab the ME may request for diabetics
CABG Coronary artery bypass graft "Remedial" — does not preclude certification; sternal healing ~3 months
CAGE Cut down / Annoyed / Guilty / Eye-opener 4-question alcohol screen; ≥ 2 "yes" suggests dependence
CBD Cannabidiol Legal if < 0.3% THC, but unregulated labeling can cause a positive (disqualifying) test
CBT Cognitive behavioral therapy PTSD/depression treatment; no certification impact itself
CDL / CLP Commercial driver's license / commercial learner's permit ≥ 26,001 lb, 16+ passengers, or placarded hazmat; subject to DOT drug testing and SDLA reporting
CF Cystic fibrosis Case-by-case; key = can driver get therapy while working
CFR Code of Federal Regulations 49 CFR 391.41–391.49 = the driver medical regulations
CHD Coronary heart disease LV function is the major prognostic factor; ETT/LVEF work-up
CHF / HF Congestive heart failure / heart failure Symptomatic HF or LVEF < 40% → do not certify (old guidance)
CM Cardiomyopathy (HCM hypertrophic, RCM restrictive) Case-by-case; cardiology input
CME Continuing medical education Not required for NR listing (only 5-yr refresher, 10-yr recert)
CMV Commercial motor vehicle ≥ 10,001 lb, > 8 pax for hire, > 15 pax not for hire, or placarded hazmat
CNS Central nervous system CNS infection/tumor → seizure risk, waiting periods under (b)(8)
COPD Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease DQ if hypoxia at rest, chronic respiratory failure, cough syncope, or needs O2
CPAP Continuous positive airway pressure OSA treatment; FMCSA sets no compliance rule — "effective, safe, stable"
CV Cardiovascular Standard (b)(4)
DEA Drug Enforcement Administration Maintains the drug schedules (21 CFR 1308)
DM / ITDM / NITDM Diabetes mellitus / insulin-treated / non-insulin-treated ITDM → 391.46, MCSA-5870, max 1 yr; NITDM → up to 2 yrs, MCSA-5872 optional
DOAC Direct oral anticoagulant Apixaban, dabigatran, etc.; no INR monitoring; judge the underlying condition
DOT Department of Transportation Parent of FMCSA; "DOT physical" = the ME exam
DQ Disqualify / disqualified Shorthand used in this sheet
DTR Deep tendon reflex Part of the neurological exam on the MER
DVT Deep vein thrombosis PE source; certify once treated and anticoagulation monitored
ECG / EKG Electrocardiogram Not a required test; ME may order; interpreted by cardiologist
ECT Electroconvulsive therapy Old guidance: 6 months symptom-free, not on maintenance ECT
EEG Electroencephalogram Seizure/sleep-disorder test; abnormal EEG raises recurrence risk
EF / LVEF (Left ventricular) ejection fraction 55–70 normal; 40–54 slightly low; 35–39 moderately; < 35 severely low; ≥ 40 to certify (old guidance)
ESRD End-stage renal disease No standard of its own → evaluate under the causative condition (DM, CV)
ETT Exercise tolerance (treadmill) test Old guidance: > 6 METs, ≥ 85% predicted HR, SBP rise ≥ 20, no ischemia; post-MI at 4–6 wks, repeat q2 yrs
FDA Food and Drug Administration CBD labeling oversight (none); drug approvals
FEV1 / FVC Forced expiratory volume in 1 s / forced vital capacity Spirometry; asthma FEV1 < 65% → no cert (old guidance)
FMCSA Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration Writes/enforces the regs; runs the National Registry; grants exemptions and SPEs
FMCSR Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations 49 CFR Parts 300–399
GAD Generalized anxiety disorder Case-by-case; benzodiazepines need prescription exception
GCWR / GVWR Gross combination / gross vehicle weight rating Weight basis for CMV (10,001) and CDL (26,001) definitions
GLP-1 Glucagon-like peptide-1 agonist (e.g., semaglutide/Ozempic) Non-insulin diabetes drug; no specific FMCSA guideline
HbA1c / A1c Glycated hemoglobin Diabetes control marker; no FMCSA cutoff; ME may request from treating provider
HCM Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy Case-by-case; sudden death may be first sign
HHS / OCR Health & Human Services / Office for Civil Rights Enforces HIPAA
HIPAA Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act No PHI release without consent; MER not to employer without driver consent
HOS Hours of service (49 CFR 395) 11 h driving / 14 h duty / 60 per 7 or 70 per 8 days
HR Heart rate Recorded with BP; ETT target ≥ 85% predicted max
HTN Hypertension Standard (b)(6); staged certification
ICD Implantable cardioverter-defibrillator Active ICD → not certifiable; disabled ICD → case-by-case (2024 MEH)
ICH / SAH Intracerebral / subarachnoid hemorrhage Old guidance: 1-yr wait (5 yrs if seizure risk)
ILD Interstitial lung disease Case-by-case; PFTs if breathless with driving tasks
INR International normalized ratio Warfarin monitoring; old guidance wants results at exam and ≥ monthly checks
IOP Intraocular pressure Normal 10–20 mmHg; glaucoma
ISO International Organization for Standardization Audiometer calibration; convert to ANSI by subtracting 14 / 10 / 8.5 dB
LOC Loss of consciousness Standard (b)(8); unknown-cause non-seizure LOC → 6-month wait recommended
LV / LVEDD / LVESD Left ventricle / end-diastolic / end-systolic dimension Valve-disease certification cutoffs (e.g., AR: LVEDD ≤ 60, LVESD ≤ 50 mm)
MAOI Monoamine oxidase inhibitor Older antidepressant class; side-effect concerns
MCSA Motor Carrier Safety Administration (form prefix) See form list below
ME Medical examiner NR-listed clinician who performs and certifies the exam; makes the final decision
MEC Medical Examiner's Certificate (MCSA-5876) Proof of qualification; driver carries it; keep copy 3 yrs
MEH Medical Examiner's Handbook FMCSA guidance document; 2024 edition current
MEP / MRB Medical Expert Panel / Medical Review Board FMCSA advisory bodies whose reports become guidance
MER Medical Examination Report (MCSA-5875) The exam record; keep 3 yrs; contains PHI
METs Metabolic equivalents Exercise capacity; > 6 METs = heavy work of commercial driving
MI Myocardial infarction Old guidance: 2-month wait, ETT, LVEF ≥ 40%, 1-yr cert
MMPI / MMSE Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory / Mini-Mental State Exam Personality test / cognitive screen
MR / MS (valve) Mitral regurgitation / mitral stenosis Symptom development is the key concern
MRI Magnetic resonance imaging Brain imaging for seizure/tumor work-up
MRO Medical review officer Physician who reviews employer DOT drug-test results — not the ME's role
MS Multiple sclerosis Case-by-case; progressive → not SPE-eligible
MSLT Multiple sleep latency test Narcolepsy diagnosis (after overnight sleep study)
MVP Mitral valve prolapse Usually benign; DQ if symptoms, arrhythmia, emboli
NOPR Notice of Proposed Removal FMCSA audit action: acknowledge in 30 days, correct in 60
NPI National Provider Identifier Not a field on the MER
NR / NRCME National Registry (of Certified Medical Examiners) FMCSA database; you get a 10-digit NR number; results uploaded by midnight next day
NRII National Registry II June 23, 2025 rule: electronic MEC transmission NR → SDLA for CDL/CLP drivers
NSVT / VT / VF Non-sustained / sustained ventricular tachycardia / fibrillation Sustained VT ≥ 30 s or LVEF < 40% with arrhythmia → DQ (old guidance)
NTSB National Transportation Safety Board Investigated the 1999 Mother's Day crash that led to the NRCME
OSA Obstructive sleep apnea Multiple risk factors → consider sleep study; treated OSA doesn't preclude
OTC Over the counter Must be listed on the MER; sedating antihistamines last 12 h
PaO2 / PaCO2 Arterial partial pressure of O2 / CO2 ABG thresholds (65 / 45 mmHg)
PAD / PVD Peripheral arterial / vascular disease Claudication; pain at rest → DQ; 3-month post-surgery wait (old)
PCI Percutaneous coronary intervention Stent; 3-week wait after elective uncomplicated PCI (old guidance)
PCP Primary care provider "Treating provider"; consult with driver consent
PE Pulmonary embolism 3 months PE-free on anticoagulation (old guidance)
PFT Pulmonary function test Indicated for lung-disease history, symptoms, smoker ≥ 35 y
PHI Protected health information HIPAA-covered; MER contents
POTS Postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome Autonomic; DQ if resting tachycardia/orthostatic BP
PTSD Post-traumatic stress disorder Case-by-case; benzos may worsen; psychiatrist input
ROM Range of motion Neck (mirrors), hips/knees > 90° (cab entry); no required values
SAP Substance abuse professional Evaluates drivers after DOT drug/alcohol violations; ME may refer but SAP eval not required for ME decision
SBP Systolic blood pressure ETT: should rise ≥ 20 mmHg
SDLA State driver licensing agency Holds CDL data and med-cert status; receives MEC (electronically under NRII)
SG Specific gravity (urine) Normal 1.005–1.030 (avg 1.020)
SGLT2 / DPP-4 Sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 / dipeptidyl peptidase-4 inhibitors Oral diabetes drug classes; evaluate side effects
SOB / DOE Shortness of breath / dyspnea on exertion Symptoms triggering respiratory or cardiac work-up
SPE Skill Performance Evaluation certificate (49 CFR 391.49) FMCSA-issued for fixed limb deficits; valid 2 yrs; renew 30 days before; driver carries with MEC
SpO2 Peripheral oxygen saturation (pulse ox) Normal 95–100%; < 92% → ABG
SSRI / SNRI Selective serotonin / serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor Not controlled; assess side effects and functioning
SVT Supraventricular tachycardia Usually not syncope-causing; ablation often curative; 1-month wait (old)
TB Tuberculosis History doesn't preclude once treated/non-infectious
TBI Traumatic brain injury Old guidance: mild 1 mo, moderate 3 yrs, severe (LOC ≥ 24 h) no; MEH: case-by-case
TCA Tricyclic antidepressant First-generation; more sedation/dizziness; assess individually
THC Tetrahydrocannabinol Marijuana's active drug; > 0.3% = Schedule I
TIA Transient ischemic attack 1 in 3 → stroke; old guidance 1-yr wait, 1-yr cert
TM Tympanic membrane Ear exam; scarred TM with 5-ft whisper other ear → certify
TWIC Transportation Worker Identification Credential Acceptable photo ID at the testing center
UA Urinalysis Required test: SG, protein, blood, sugar (dipstick)
VA Veterans Affairs VA-certified MEs may examine veteran drivers only
WPW Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome Accessory-pathway SVT; certifiable after treatment/clearance (old guidance)

FMCSA forms (MCSA numbers)

Form Name Who fills it / why
MCSA-5850 CMV Driver Medical Examination Results ME uploads to National Registry by midnight of the next calendar day; also used to update "determination pending"
MCSA-5870 Insulin-Treated Diabetes Mellitus Assessment Treating clinician; required for ITDM; ME exam within 45 days
MCSA-5871 Vision Evaluation Report Ophthalmologist/optometrist; required for the Alternative Vision Standard; within 45 days
MCSA-5872 Non-Insulin-Treated Diabetes Assessment Optional information form
MCSA-5875 Medical Examination Report (MER) Driver (Section 1) + ME (Section 2); keep 3 years
MCSA-5876 Medical Examiner's Certificate (MEC) ME and driver sign; driver carries; keep copy 3 years
MCSA-5895 CMV Driver Medication Form Optional; prescriber explains why a medication was prescribed and whether it impairs driving
