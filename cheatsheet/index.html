Cheat sheet

The whole exam on a few dozen pages

Every fact is tagged by source: reg regulation text, MEH24 the 2024 Handbook, legacy pre-2024 FMCSA tables, clinical standard clinical references, and training for common training-course conventions that are not FMCSA text. Where a traditional number and the 2024 Handbook differ, both are shown and labeled.

Seizures, Loss of Consciousness & Diabetes
Epilepsy and single seizures, the seizure exemption, loss of consciousness, vertigo; the insulin-treated standard and non-insulin diabetes.
Section 7

Mental-status assessment and condition tables; drug use, marijuana and CBD, DOT testing, DEA schedules; alcoholism; medications.

Forms, Recordkeeping & Exam Prep
Who performs the exam; MER, MEC and the other MCSA forms; required tests; determinations; recordkeeping; the certification test itself.

Acronym Glossary
Every abbreviation used in the guide, plus the MCSA form numbers.
