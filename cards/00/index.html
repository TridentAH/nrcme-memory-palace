What this card covers

Who needs a DOT physical — CMV and CDL thresholds, passengers, hazmat, interstate, the two-year ceiling, hours of service. Standard: 49 CFR 390.5 · 383.5 · 391.45 · 395.3.

The hooks to keep

  • 10,001 lb makes a CMV; 26,001 lb makes a CDL
  • 9 for hire or 16 for free — both include the driver
  • Placarded hazmat always counts
  • Two years is the ceiling
  • Interstate just means crossing the line
  • Age 21, English, and the road test are the carrier's job
